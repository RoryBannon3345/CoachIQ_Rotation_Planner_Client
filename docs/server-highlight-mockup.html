Server highlight — mockup

The record screen highlights the player who should serve. It works this out from the set's planned rotation (sent by the Planner in roster contract 6) and the rallies logged so far. These are static frames of the real screen; nothing here changes the app. What's new is the blue row with the accent bar.

Set 1 plan from the Planner: 1Grace2Addison3Hailey4Brynn5Jade6Kara · Ivy on the bench

1 · Following the plan one look, whether we serve or receive

The rule: while we serve, the row is the server of this rally. While they serve, it's our next server, the player who serves once we win the ball back. That way the right row is already marked when the first serve of a turn is tapped. The rotation moves only when we win the ball back, as on the Planner's Rally replay.

2 · A different player serves the phone follows the coach

The rule: only the first Serve tap of each of our serve turns can change the plan. If it names someone else: (1) she's the planned player coming back for her stand-in → "back in"; (2) she's elsewhere in the order → the rotation re-aligns to her; (3) she's not in the order → she stands in for the planned player in that spot for the rest of the set. Each change is its own Undo step (↶ Undo re-align), so Undo takes back the change and keeps the serve stat. A minus on that tap (the "wrong row" fix) also takes it back.

3 · No highlight when the phone can't know

No highlight when: the roster came from an older Planner (no plan), the set has a typed score, serve-first isn't answered, the plan has an empty spot there, or the planned player isn't ticked for the set.
