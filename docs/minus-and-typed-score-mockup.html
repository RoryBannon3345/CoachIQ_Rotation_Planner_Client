Minus cancels a rally & typing a final score — mockup

Two follow-ups to auto-scoring (4.3.0), both answered "yes" by Rory. Static frames of the real record screen; nothing here changes the app.

1 · A minus on the tap that opened the rally cancels it the "wrong row" fix

The rule: a minus on the same player's In counter that opened the rally still open closes that rally, and the toast says so. A minus anywhere else only fixes the count, as today. Undo of the minus re-opens the rally. Undo of the original tap still works too and is the quickest fix when you notice straight away.

2 · Type the final score on a set that already has a rally log

The rule: a set with a log now offers Type the final score… beside Clear points. The sheet opens on the log's tally, says the log will be replaced, and Replace log swaps it for the typed score in one step (counts stay). Like Clear points, Undo cannot bring the log back, which is why the sheet asks first.
