Roster density — fitting 12 players without scrolling

All three phones below are drawn at a true 375 × 812 CSS viewport (iPhone X / 11 Pro / 13 mini class, which is what the screenshot shows), with the same 12-player roster in each. The only differences are the browser chrome and the row density.

The constraint is the tap target, not the row. Apple's minimum touch target is 44×44pt. Squeezing 12 rows into a browser tab forces ~36px targets — and a mis-tap here writes a wrong stat into a coach's only copy. Reclaiming the browser's own chrome is worth 82px, which is most of what is needed, and costs no target size at all.

How the compact mode works

  • Rows stop being a fixed height and become a grid that fills the space: grid-auto-rows: minmax(38px, 1fr). Eight players get roomy 68px rows; twelve compress to fit; a thirteenth would scroll rather than shrink past the 38px floor. Nothing is hardcoded to a player count.
  • The chrome gives back ~17px — topbar 48→44, set tabs 44→38, column header and bottom bar padding trimmed. The Undo / − / Export buttons keep their full 44px, since those are the ones hit under pressure.
  • Nothing below 38px. That floor is deliberate: past it the target stops being reliably hittable, and I would rather the coach scroll than mis-record.
